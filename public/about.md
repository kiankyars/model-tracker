## Coverage

The catalog combines [Models.dev](https://models.dev/) model metadata with historical releases and source-backed corrections maintained in [this repository](https://github.com/kiankyars/model-tracker). It includes text-generating models and multimodal models that generate text. It excludes embedding-only models, rerankers, image-only generators, and anonymous routing endpoints. Research models, regional releases, fine-tunes, and models without API distribution are less consistently represented.

## Updates and corrections

To report a missing model or incorrect field, [open an issue](https://github.com/kiankyars/model-tracker/issues/new) with a primary source, the model identifier, and the proposed correction.

## Privacy

The site has no analytics, account system, cookies, or third-party scripts.
